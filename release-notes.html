Latest
Version 1.0 September 2026
  • Initial release: play 31 (also known as Scat or Blitz) solo against AI opponents, pass-and-play style.
  • Adjustable house rules, including an Extra Wild card, a Blind Knock Round, and an optional knock penalty.
  • Chips and dollar amounts are for scorekeeping only - no real money is ever used, wagered, or won.
  • Compact, phone-friendly table for landscape play, alongside the classic full table view.
  • A guided in-app tour walks new players through setup and their first hand.
  • Built-in Full Rules reference and a Check My Math discard warning to help you avoid costly mistakes.
  • Undo option for your most recent action, plus custom bot names and card back themes.
