Common questions

Is this real-money gambling?
No. Chips and dollar amounts in 31 are for scorekeeping only — no real money is ever used, wagered, or won.
Do I need an internet connection?
No. Every game runs entirely on your device against local bots or pass-and-play friends. Nothing is sent anywhere.
The app looks frozen or a control isn't responding — what should I do?
Try fully closing and reopening the app first. If that doesn't fix it, email a quick description of what happened (and your device model) and it'll get looked into.
Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?
Absolutely — that's what the email above is for.
