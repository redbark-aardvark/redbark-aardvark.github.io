Privacy Policy

The short version: this app doesn't collect anything. Here's the full explanation.

Last updated September 2026

What we collect

Nothing. 31 does not collect, transmit, store remotely, or share any personal data, usage data, analytics, or device identifiers. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your game settings (players, chip counts, themes, and similar preferences) and any in-progress game are saved locally on your device only, using standard on-device storage. This information never leaves your device, is never sent to us, and is never shared with anyone else.

No accounts, no tracking

  • No sign-up or account creation of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No third-party tracking of any kind.
  • No in-app purchases.

Everything about how the game plays runs entirely on your device.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes - for example, if a future version adds a feature that requires collecting data - this page will be updated first, and the change will be reflected in the app's next update notes.
